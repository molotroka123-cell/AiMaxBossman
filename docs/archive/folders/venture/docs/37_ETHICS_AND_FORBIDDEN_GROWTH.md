# Ethics and forbidden growth

Forbidden regardless of projected revenue:
- fake reviews;
- fabricated testimonials;
- fake customers;
- circular self-payments counted as traction;
- misleading scarcity;
- impersonation;
- deceptive government affiliation;
- spam;
- covert scraping bypass;
- unlawful personal-data acquisition;
- hiding material product limitations;
- manipulation of metrics.

## Principle

Revenue obtained by destroying trust or creating hidden legal liability is not
positive Venture Engine performance.

Contribution metrics should include refunds, chargebacks and incident costs.
