# Financial control model

## Ledgers

Maintain logically separate:
- legal entity;
- venture;
- channel;
- experiment.

## Revenue integrity

Paid:
money from external customer for product/service.

Do not count:
- owner transfers;
- test payments;
- refundable deposit until treatment is defined;
- VAT as revenue;
- cancelled/refunded invoice as retained revenue.

## Cost integrity

Direct variable:
charge to venture.

Shared:
allocate using transparent rule.

Unknown:
do not hide; place in unallocated cost bucket.

## Cash vs P&L

Report both.

A venture may look profitable while receivables remain unpaid.

## Tax reserve

Future implementation should allow configurable tax reserve but not pretend to
calculate final tax liability without current rules/accountant input.
