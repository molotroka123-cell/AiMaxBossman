# Venture autonomy levels

## L0 — Analyst

Research, score, calculate, draft.
No external action.

## L1 — Experiment assistant

Prepares landing pages, campaigns, outreach drafts, documents.
Human executes/publishes/sends.

## L2 — Bounded operator

May operate a pre-approved experiment inside:
- fixed channel;
- fixed total budget;
- fixed duration;
- fixed audience;
- fixed stop rules.

## L3 — Portfolio operator

May reallocate small pre-approved budgets among already approved experiments.

Requires mature audit trail.

## L4 — High autonomy

Possible only after repeated safe, profitable operation.

Still not autonomous:
- loans;
- arbitrary bank transfers;
- company formation;
- major contracts;
- tax elections;
- major new budgets.

## Promotion criteria

Autonomy increases only with:
- audit history;
- low incident rate;
- metric integrity;
- bounded-loss controls;
- reversible actions;
- demonstrated contribution.
