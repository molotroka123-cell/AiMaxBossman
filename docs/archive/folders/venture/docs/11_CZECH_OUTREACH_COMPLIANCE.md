# Czech outbound/commercial communication boundary

This document is a product-control policy, not legal advice.

Current ÚOOÚ guidance states:
- an electronic contact publicly available on the Internet cannot simply be
  used to send advertising;
- sending an unsolicited electronic request asking for permission to send
  advertising is not a valid workaround;
- existing-customer communications for own similar products/services have
  statutory conditions and must provide clear opt-out.

Therefore default Venture Engine policy:

## DENY
- scrape public e-mails -> mass commercial campaign;
- purchase opaque scraped list -> mass campaign;
- consent-request spam;
- fake “service notice” carrying promotion.

## ASK / legal basis required
- external commercial e-mail/SMS;
- bulk messaging;
- campaign using existing-customer exception.

## Preferred
- opt-in;
- double opt-in where suitable;
- inbound response;
- SEO;
- paid ads;
- referral;
- partner channel;
- marketplace;
- tender/RFQ response.

## Required records
- contact source;
- consent/customer status;
- purpose;
- opt-out;
- suppression list;
- campaign identity;
- last legal review.

Public-registry data may be used for market analysis and verification without
automatically authorizing outreach.
