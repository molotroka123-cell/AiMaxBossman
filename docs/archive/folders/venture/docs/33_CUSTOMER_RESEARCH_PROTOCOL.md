# Customer research protocol

## Interview objective

Understand current behavior and money/time cost.

Do not pitch first.

## Ask

- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- What did you do?
- How long?
- Who else was involved?
- What software/document?
- What broke?
- What did it cost?
- How often?
- Have you paid to solve it?
- What happens if you do nothing?

## Avoid

“Would you use an AI app that...?”

## Qualification

Record:
- role;
- business type;
- size;
- frequency;
- current tools;
- payer authority.

## Evidence coding

Quote themes independently.
Record contradictions.

## Payment

At end of appropriate research:
offer a concrete paid pilot.

Payment behavior is separate evidence from interview enthusiasm.
