# BOSSMAN Venture Engine — Foundation V1.0

**Purpose:** build the business constitution for a future agent feature that
discovers, validates, launches, measures, scales, pivots and kills ventures.

**This ZIP intentionally contains no product implementation code.**

The work in this package is business-first:
- Czech market evidence;
- opportunity scoring;
- unit economics;
- legal/commercial operating boundaries;
- stage gates;
- capital allocation;
- experiment design;
- portfolio governance;
- company-formation triggers;
- Opus decision protocol;
- templates and machine-readable schemas.

The goal is not to make an agent "invent businesses".
The goal is to make an agent allocate time and capital better than ad-hoc
human brainstorming.

## Frozen policy defaults

Unless the owner changes them explicitly:

- first geographic market: **Czech Republic**
- active build-stage ventures: **1**
- additional active research-stage venture: **1**
- ordinary validation experiment budget: **5,000 CZK**
- external pre-payment venture build cap: **20,000 CZK**
- >50,000 CZK cumulative external venture spend: mandatory investment review
- autonomous cold outbound: **DENY**
- new outbound commercial communication: **ASK**
- ad campaigns: human-approved budget/cap first
- bank transfers/new beneficiaries/loans/contracts/company formation: **ASK or DENY**
- s.r.o. transition review: **100,000 CZK MRR for 3 consecutive months**
  or earlier when liability, enterprise contracts, IP, hiring, VAT proximity or
  investment trigger it
- ŽivnoPilot is a calibration candidate, **not a predetermined winner**
- no product should be scaled before real payment evidence unless a written
  exception explains why.

## Fundamental principle

```
interesting idea
    ↓
evidence
    ↓
business hypothesis
    ↓
cheapest falsifiable experiment
    ↓
real willingness to pay
    ↓
build only what evidence justifies
    ↓
retention + margin + acquisition
    ↓
scale / pivot / kill
```

No code-first ventures.
