# Hair recolour v2 - fixed thresholds (written 11.10.2026 BEFORE the first v2 run)

Owner defect clips (v1 promos): flat fill without hair texture, recolour bleeding onto forehead / temples / neck, jagged edge,
"platinum" came out grey-green, colour flicker, RGB-split on the face, titles next to the head.

## Method (tools/trend_edit/hair_v2.py)
1. SegFormer-B2 (ATR) per frame: hair, skin (face+arms+legs), person - the existing `parsing_worker` probabilities.
2. RobustVideoMatting person alpha (ONNX, recurrent -> temporally stable) - the recolour can never leave the person (leak).
3. Hair alpha = guided filter (radius ~3 px, colour+luma guide) of the SegFormer hair map, multiplied by the RVM person alpha,
   minus skin: face/skin probability and pixels that are skin-coloured and brighter than the hair are removed.
4. Recolour in Lab, NOT a flat fill: L' = target_mean + k*(L - mean_L), k chosen so the std of L inside the hair does not
   fall below 0.8x the original; highlights (top 10% of L) are kept as a soft specular add; a/b pulled to the target.
5. Temporal smoothing: EMA of the alpha (0.6) + EMA of the per-frame mean-L offset.

## Thresholds (per clip, measured on every frame that has hair; PASS needs all)
| Id | Metric | Definition | PASS |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | edge | mean distance (px, at source resolution) from the alpha=0.5 contour to the nearest source-image edge (Canny on luma), contour points with no edge within 8 px excluded and their share reported | <= 2 px |
| L | leak | share of changed pixels (dE76 > 4 vs source) that lie outside the RVM person alpha (> 0.5, dilated 2 px) | <= 1 % |
| S | skin | share of SegFormer skin/face pixels (eroded 2 px) whose colour changed by dE76 > 4 | <= 0.5 % |
| V | texture | std of L inside the hair: recoloured / original | >= 0.8 |
| C | colour | mean Lab inside the hair vs the target colour (platinum target L 80 a 0 b 6), dE76 | <= 15 (platinum), reported for all |
| T | flicker | mean abs frame-to-frame change of mean hair Lab minus the same for the source | reported |

Edge and leak are measured against signals independent of the hair mask itself (image edges, RVM alpha). Neither is a ground
truth matte; a human-made matte does not exist for these clips - stated, not hidden.
Time box: 45 min. If a threshold is not met, the best version ships and the miss is reported.
