# Personal research -> OSVČ -> s.r.o.

## Principle

Research can begin personally.

Recurring commercial sale should move through the appropriate Czech
business/legal framework rather than being designed as informal personal
transfers.

## LegalEntity abstraction

Required from future implementation:
- entity type;
- name;
- IČO;
- DIČ;
- VAT status;
- invoice identity;
- banking/payment identity reference;
- effective date;
- accounting export;
- contract identity.

## Default s.r.o. review triggers

- >=100,000 CZK MRR for 3 consecutive months;
- >=1.2m CZK annualized revenue;
- first material long-term hire/contractor;
- meaningful product liability;
- enterprise customer requires DPA/SLA/company counterparty;
- 2+ meaningful revenue products;
- investor/partner;
- VAT proximity;
- IP becomes saleable;
- risk-isolation need.

These are management triggers, not statutory mandates.

## Required migration memo

- OSVČ economics;
- s.r.o. economics;
- tax assumptions;
- accounting/admin burden;
- liability;
- IP transfer;
- contracts;
- VAT;
- hiring;
- customer requirements;
- transition costs;
- accountant/lawyer questions;
- recommendation/confidence.

Current public BusinessInfo information states minimum s.r.o. registered
capital can be 1 CZK. That does not make formation economically costless.
