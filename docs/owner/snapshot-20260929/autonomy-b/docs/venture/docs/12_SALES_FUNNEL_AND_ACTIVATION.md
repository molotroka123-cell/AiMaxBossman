# Sales funnel and activation

Default funnel:

visitor
→ lead
→ qualified
→ demo/trial
→ activated
→ paid
→ retained
→ expanded/referral.

## Diagnostic rule

When growth fails identify the stage.

No traffic:
distribution problem.

Traffic but no lead:
positioning/offer problem.

Lead but no activation:
onboarding/product problem.

Activated but no payment:
pricing/value problem.

Paid but churn:
retention/quality problem.

## Early operating targets

These are internal targets, not universal market facts.

Relevant visitor -> lead:
- <2% after meaningful sample: investigate;
- 2–5% plausible;
- >5% strong in high-intent niche.

Self-serve trial:
- activation >=40% target;
- trial-to-paid >=15% baseline;
- >=25% strong with well-qualified traffic.

Do not make decisions from tiny samples without uncertainty.
