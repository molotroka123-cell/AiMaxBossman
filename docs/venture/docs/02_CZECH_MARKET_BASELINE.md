# Czech market baseline

Observed date: 2026-08-28.

## Structural thesis

Czechia is attractive for a first venture portfolio because:
- the economy contains a very large micro/SME base;
- many workflows are local/regulatory/language specific;
- official digital infrastructure is relatively strong;
- advanced business digitalisation still has gaps;
- regulation creates recurring deadlines and forced process changes.

## Verified current signals

### Digitalisation

European Commission, Czechia 2026 Digital Decade Country Report:
Czechia has solid digital foundations but material gaps remain in SME
digitalisation, advanced-technology uptake, interoperability and data sharing.

This supports products that connect fragmented processes rather than merely
adding a chat interface.

### VAT

Finanční správa currently describes two turnover thresholds relevant to
statutory Czech VAT registration monitoring:
- 2,000,000 CZK;
- 2,536,500 CZK.

The difference matters for timing of payer status.

These values are volatile legal facts. They must be revalidated before any
customer-facing tax recommendation.

### Corporate income tax

General Czech corporate income-tax rate currently stated by Finanční správa:
21%, subject to statutory exceptions.

### s.r.o.

BusinessInfo currently states:
- one founder is possible;
- minimum registered capital can be 1 CZK;
- lowest possible contribution can be 1 CZK.

This is a legal formation fact, not an argument that creating a company is
economically free.

### EET 2.0

Finanční správa:
- technical developer documentation published in June 2026;
- Playground opened 1 July 2026;
- planned DIS+ functions: 1 Nov 2026;
- planned MOJE EET: 1 Dec 2026;
- planned pilot: Jan 2027;
- planned production: Feb 2027.

Legislative uncertainty remains:
Parliamentary history for bill 189 shows the Senate returned the proposal with
amendments on 19 August 2026.

Therefore:
TECHNICAL ROADMAP != FINAL LAW.

## OSVČ/SME sizing

The historical source draft uses the ČSSZ March-2026 OSVČ dataset and the
European Commission 2025 SME fact sheet.

Before presenting exact total counts in an investor/customer document, the
agent must re-open the primary datasets and record:
- publication;
- reporting date;
- exact total;
- definition;
- whether inactive entities are excluded.

The Venture Engine should never use the entire OSVČ population as SAM.

TAM -> SAM -> SOM must be segmented by:
- profession;
- employee count;
- workflow frequency;
- software adoption;
- payment ability;
- geography;
- regulatory scope.

## Market-selection preference

Prefer niches where:
- customer loses measurable time/money;
- workflow occurs at least weekly/monthly;
- payer is obvious;
- first pilot needs <10 customers;
- distribution does not require unlawful mass outreach;
- integration burden is bounded;
- gross margin can exceed 70%.
