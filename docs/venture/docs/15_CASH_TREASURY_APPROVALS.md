# Cash, treasury and approval policy

## AUTO

May:
- read/reconcile authorized financial data;
- calculate cashflow;
- classify product costs;
- forecast;
- prepare payment/refund proposals.

## ASK

- outbound payment;
- new beneficiary;
- refund;
- ad campaign/new cap;
- vendor subscription;
- contractor;
- invoice sending until identity/control rules are established;
- tax payment;
- domain purchase;
- material customer credit.

## DENY

- loan;
- arbitrary asset speculation;
- self-authorized bank-account creation;
- circular payments to fabricate traction;
- moving customer funds without explicit permitted business flow.

## Approval card

Must show:
- action;
- amount;
- beneficiary;
- venture;
- legal effect;
- data leaving system;
- reversible?
- expected metric;
- stop condition.

Example:
Approve 3,000 CZK search experiment for 7 days?
Target <=250 CZK qualified lead.
Stop after 1,500 CZK if zero qualified leads.
