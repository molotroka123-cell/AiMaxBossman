# App #3 selection protocol

App #3 must not be chosen because it sounds coolest.

## Candidate set

Before selection:
- generate 20 opportunities across at least 8 distinct industries;
- no more than 3 may be close variants of the same workflow;
- at least 5 must not rely on camera/vision;
- at least 5 must not rely on 3D/manufacturing;
- at least 5 should derive from Czech-specific regulatory/administrative
  fragmentation;
- include service-to-software candidates.

## Shortlist

Score all 20.
Select top 5 by final score after confidence.

For top 5:
- competitor pricing;
- buyer;
- legal route;
- validation experiment;
- 12-month bear/base/bull;
- development-month estimate.

## Final 3

Devil's Advocate pass.
Opus ranks by:
risk-adjusted contribution per development month.

## Winner

Owner approves one validation candidate.

Winner does NOT enter full implementation.
It enters Stage 1/2 validation.

## Diversity

App #1 and App #2 should not bias App #3 toward another hardware workflow.
A materially different economic domain is preferred if score is comparable.
