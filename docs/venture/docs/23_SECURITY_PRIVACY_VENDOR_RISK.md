# Security, privacy and vendor economics

Before selecting a venture estimate:
- personal-data volume;
- sensitive-data category;
- DPA needs;
- retention;
- breach impact;
- hosting;
- vendor dependence.

## Economic principle

Security/privacy burden is part of venture cost.

A smaller non-sensitive market can be superior to a larger regulated market
for an early portfolio.

## Vendor risk

For each critical vendor:
- function;
- price;
- variable/fixed;
- data held;
- export;
- lock-in;
- fallback;
- outage impact;
- price-increase impact.

Warning:
one vendor can disable core service with no fallback.

## AI vendor

Track per account:
- calls;
- input/output;
- cost;
- latency;
- error;
- fallback;
- quality KPI.

Do not save money by silently degrading customer outcome.
