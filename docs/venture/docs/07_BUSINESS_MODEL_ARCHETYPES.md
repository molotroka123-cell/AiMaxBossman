# Business model archetypes

## Micro-SaaS
199–999 CZK/month.
Self-serve, low support, volume acquisition.

## Vertical SMB SaaS
1,000–10,000 CZK/month.
Onboarding/integrations, lower volume, higher switching cost.

## Usage-based AI
Base subscription + usage.
Inference/document/storage cost explicitly variable.

## Service-to-software
Manual/AI-assisted delivery first, then automate repeated work.
Preferred for uncertain B2B workflows.

## Monitoring/data
Subscription for alerts/risk/compliance.

## Transaction/revenue share
Only when payment/legal architecture is clear.

## Internal-to-external
Solve a real internal workflow, validate externally, then spin out.

Selection criterion:
risk-adjusted contribution margin per development month.
