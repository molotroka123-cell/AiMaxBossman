# Retention, churn and expansion

## Retention event

Must represent real value, not login.

Examples:
- completed job;
- reconciled payment;
- delivered report;
- processed document;
- scheduled service;
- monitored compliance event.

## Cohorts

Show M0, M1, M2, M3, M6, M12.

Do not blend new and old accounts into one comforting number.

## Churn taxonomy

- no value;
- price;
- missing workflow;
- business closed;
- seasonal;
- competitor;
- technical;
- support;
- compliance/privacy;
- involuntary payment failure.

Prioritize by lost contribution MRR.

## Expansion

Track:
- seat;
- location;
- volume;
- higher tier;
- add-on.

Do not rely on expansion to hide poor base retention.
